# Reports

## Scope finance enrollment metrics to course-item value
Gross and Net Enrollment metrics include only fulfilled course order items in the selected academic term. Net deducts discounts, eligible non-gift-card/non-expired/non-revoked store credit actually used on those course items, and successful refunds; never deduct credit merely because it was issued during the term or allocated to gear/other products. Eligible store credit is deducted in full when applied, including for payment-plan enrollments; it is not prorated with paid installments.
