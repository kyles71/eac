# EAC Plié Portal User Guide and Review

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26 | Application area: User panel (`/dancefam`)

## Purpose and scope

This document has two audiences:

1. **Families and portal users:** Part I explains every page in the EAC Plié Portal, how to complete common tasks, and what important actions affect.
2. **EAC owners and staff:** Part II records issues and pain points found during the review, followed by possible future improvements and open product questions.

The guide reflects the current application behavior and a review of the portal at desktop and mobile widths. Exact class dates, payment terms, forms, notices, products, and available actions vary by account and by EAC's current configuration.

# Part I: User guide

## 1. Getting started

### Registering for an account

Choose **Register** from the sign-in page and enter the parent or guardian's:

- first and last name;
- email address; and
- password.

If portal terms are available, you must accept them before registering. Select the terms link to read the current document before accepting it.

The account should belong to the adult who manages the family's registrations and payments. Add dancers as students after creating the account; do not create a separate account for each child.

### Signing in and resetting a password

Sign in with the account email address and password. **Remember me** keeps the account signed in on the current browser, so use it only on a trusted device.

If the password is forgotten, choose **Forgot your password?**, submit the account email, and follow the reset link sent by email.

### Main navigation

The portal contains these main areas:

- **My Dashboard** — an overview of items needing attention, upcoming events, announcements, and the next payment.
- **Calendar** — EAC class, event, closure, and holiday dates available to the account.
- **My Classes** — purchased class seats and their assigned students.
- **My Forms** — forms and waivers that need to be signed, plus completed and expired records.
- **Store** — classes, events, merchandise, gift cards, and other products currently for sale.
- **Cart** — products being prepared for checkout. A number beside it is the total quantity in the cart.
- **Students** — dancers connected to the family account.
- **Billing** — orders, receipts, payment plans, credits, gift cards, and saved payment methods.
- **Messages** — a history of email sent to the account email address.

On a small screen, open the navigation menu to reach these pages.

### Account menu

Open the profile menu in the upper-right corner to reach **My Profile**, security settings, and **Sign out**. Staff members who also have administrative access may see an **Admin** link here; ordinary family accounts do not.

### Notices and badges

Notices can appear above the page content throughout the portal:

- EAC announcements may include a link or a close button.
- Form notices identify missing waivers or other required forms.
- Class notices identify purchased seats that have not yet been assigned to a student.

Closing a dismissible EAC announcement hides it for the account, not just for the current visit. There is currently no user-facing archive from which to restore a dismissed announcement.

A badge beside **My Forms** is the number of active forms still waiting for completion. A badge beside **Cart** is the total number of products in the cart.

## 2. Recommended first-time setup

New families should complete these steps:

1. Open **Students** and add each dancer.
2. Open **My Profile** and confirm the account name and email.
3. Set up two-factor authentication under **My Profile** for stronger account security.
4. Add a saved payment method in **Billing**, if desired.
5. Review **My Forms** and complete anything pending.
6. Review **My Classes** and assign a student to every open seat.

## 3. My Dashboard

The dashboard is the portal's starting point. It combines several types of information so urgent work can be found without visiting every page.

### Needs Attention

This area can show:

- unassigned class seats;
- pending medical waivers;
- other pending required forms; and
- links to resolve each item.

An **open seat** means the class or event has been purchased, but no student is assigned to that enrollment. It does not mean the seat is available for someone else to buy.

### Messages From EAC

This area contains active portal announcements. These are different from the delivered-email records on the **Messages** page.

### Quick Links

Quick links provide shortcuts to frequently used areas such as students, forms, classes, the store, or billing.

### Coming Up

This area lists up to five accessible calendar events occurring during the next 30 days. Use **Calendar** when a complete schedule or more event detail is needed.

### Next Payment

If the account has an upcoming payment-plan installment, this area shows the next scheduled amount and date. Open **Billing** to see the full plan and installment schedule.

### Header shortcuts

Depending on screen size, shortcuts are available for:

- browsing the store;
- viewing the calendar;
- adding a student; and
- opening billing.

## 4. Calendar

The calendar is a read-only schedule of events available to the account. Portal users cannot create, edit, move, or cancel events.

### Changing the calendar display

Use the calendar selector to choose among calendars the account is allowed to view. Available views include:

- list;
- month;
- week; and
- day.

Use **Previous**, **Next**, and **Today** to move through dates.

### Viewing an event

Select a class or event to open its details. The detail window can include:

- event and calendar name;
- related course or focus;
- start and end time;
- description; and
- cancellation information.

Holiday entries are informational and do not open a detail window.

### Buying from the calendar

When an event is connected to a product, its detail window may show:

- **Add to Cart** when the item can be purchased directly;
- **View in Store** when more product information or purchaser questions must be reviewed; or
- a sold-out state when capacity has been reached.

Adding an item from the calendar has the same effect as adding it from the store.

## 5. My Classes

**My Classes** lists class and event enrollments purchased by the account. It is also where seats are assigned to dancers.

### Finding an enrollment

Use the semester tabs—such as **Winter/Spring**, **Summer**, **Fall**, **Past**, or **All**—to change the time period. Search can narrow the list, and the **Student** filter can show enrollments assigned to a particular dancer.

The status describes the enrollment's current timing or state. Select a row to see course details.

### Assigning an open seat

To assign a purchased seat:

1. Open the enrollment's action menu.
2. Choose **Assign Student**.
3. Select an existing student, or create a student in the assignment window.
4. Save the assignment.
5. Follow any links to newly required forms.

Assigning a student can create medical-waiver or other form requirements for that student. Check **My Forms** after making an assignment.

### Changing the assigned student

If changes are still allowed:

1. Open the enrollment's action menu.
2. Choose **Change Student**.
3. Select the replacement student and save.

The purchase, price, and class seat remain the same. Only the dancer assigned to the seat changes.

Changing the student causes the portal to recalculate which forms are required. Pending forms that are no longer needed for the former student may be removed. Previously completed forms are retained.

### Removing a student from an enrollment

If changes are still allowed, choose **Remove Student** and confirm the action.

> **Important:** Removing the student does not cancel the enrollment and does not issue a refund. The purchased seat remains on the account and becomes an open, unassigned seat.

Removing an assignment can also remove pending forms that are no longer required for that student. Completed records are retained.

### Assignment cutoff

Student assignments can be changed or removed only before EAC's configured cutoff ahead of the first meeting. The current default is seven days. Once the cutoff is reached, the actions no longer appear. Email [eacdance@outlook.com](mailto:eacdance@outlook.com) if a correction is required after that point.

## 6. Students

The Students area stores the dancers managed by the family account.

### Adding a student

Choose **Add Student**, then enter:

- first name;
- last name;
- date of birth; and
- nickname, if used.

First name, last name, and birth date are required. The date of birth is used to calculate the student's age and may affect forms or enrollment eligibility.

### Student list

The list shows each student's name, nickname, birth date, and class count. Select a student to open the full record.

### Student details

The student page includes:

- identifying information and calculated age;
- nickname;
- additional email addresses;
- medical-waiver status and actions;
- courses and events; and
- enrollment history.

First name, last name, and birth date cannot be edited by a portal user after creation. Email [eacdance@outlook.com](mailto:eacdance@outlook.com) when one of those values needs correction.

### Nickname and additional emails

To update these details:

1. Change the nickname as needed.
2. Add, change, or remove additional email rows.
3. Choose **Save Student Details**.

The save replaces the student's saved additional-email list with the rows currently shown. Removing a row and saving removes that address from the student.

Additional student emails are contact recipients, not separate portal accounts, and cannot be used to sign in. When EAC sends an email to a student, it is sent to the account holder and every additional email saved for that student. The account holder's copy appears in **Messages**; additional recipients do not have their own portal history.

### Medical waiver

The student page identifies whether the student has a current medical waiver and when it was last updated. Available actions can include:

- **Complete** — create a needed waiver;
- **View** — read the current completed waiver; and
- **Update** — create a newly signed revision.

A medical-waiver update does not silently alter the old signed record. It creates a new revision and requires the information, consents, dates, and signature to be confirmed again.

### Courses, events, and enrollment history

The courses and events section provides the student's linked activity records. Enrollment history starts with the most recent entries and loads more as the list is viewed.

### Deleting a student

A student can be deleted only when the student has no enrollments. The action requires confirmation.

> **Important:** Deleting an eligible student is permanent. Once a student has any enrollment history, the delete action is unavailable.

## 7. My Forms

This page contains forms assigned to the account or its students.

### Form tabs

- **Pending** — active forms awaiting completion.
- **Completed** — signed or submitted forms.
- **Expired** — forms whose valid period has ended.

Counts on the tabs help identify outstanding work. The form list can also expose optional columns such as signature, created date, and updated date.

Select a pending form to complete it. Select a completed form to read its submitted contents.

### Completing a student medical waiver

The waiver is normally completed once per student per EAC dance year. For a student under 18, it must be signed by a parent or legal guardian.

The form asks for:

- the student and signer's relationship;
- home address;
- at least one emergency contact;
- emergency-contact phone and optional email;
- whether an emergency contact accepts text-message updates;
- allergies, conditions, injuries, and medications;
- optional behavioral notes;
- medical-treatment consent;
- health and safety policy consent;
- media consent;
- consent dates; and
- the signer's typed signature and date.

Use `N/A` in required medical-detail fields when there is nothing to disclose. Review linked policies before consenting, then save the form.

### Updating a medical waiver

Choose **Update** from the latest current waiver when medical or contact information changes. The portal copies the existing answers into a new revision, but the signer must review the information and provide fresh consent dates and a new signature.

The previous completed waiver is preserved for history. Older or expired waivers may be viewable without offering the update action.

### Other forms

Other assigned forms, such as participation forms, can have their own questions and signatures. If an **Update** action is available, review all answers before saving.

Unlike a medical-waiver revision, updating another form currently changes the existing form record rather than preserving a separate signed revision.

## 8. Store

The store contains products currently offered by EAC, including classes, events, merchandise, gift cards, or other configured items.

### Browsing

Use search to find a product by its visible information. Switch between list and card views with the view control; the account remembers the selection.

The list can show product name, description, price, and available capacity. Products that do not use capacity display **N/A** for spots. A sold-out product cannot be added.

On a phone, card view is generally easier to use than list view because list columns and the add button may require horizontal scrolling.

### Product details

Select a product to see its full description and images. Class or event products may also include:

- semester;
- starting date;
- duration;
- teacher;
- remaining spots; and
- prerequisite information.

### Adding a product to the cart

Choose **Add to Cart**. Depending on the product, the portal may first request:

- purchaser-specific questions;
- information for each unit being purchased; or
- a custom gift-card amount.

Answers to purchaser questions become part of the order. If more than one unit is purchased, each unit can have its own answers.

## 9. Cart

The cart is where quantities, purchaser details, discount codes, gift cards, credit, and payment choices are prepared before checkout.

### Editing products

For each cart line:

- use **+** to increase quantity;
- use **−** to decrease quantity;
- use the delete action to remove it after confirmation; and
- choose **Edit Details** to change purchaser answers when the product requires them.

The cart will not proceed to payment while required purchaser answers are incomplete.

### Discount codes

Enter a discount code in the promo or gift-card field and apply it. The discount appears in the order summary when it is valid for the cart.

Discount selection currently belongs to the active cart-page session. Refreshing or navigating away can require the code to be entered again.

### Redeeming a gift card

Gift-card codes use the same entry field as discount codes.

> **Important:** Applying a valid gift-card code redeems the card to this account's store-credit balance immediately. It is not merely attached temporarily to the current order. The gift-card code is consumed and cannot then be given to another person.

After redeeming an unrestricted gift card, turn on **Apply store credit** if that balance should be used for the order. Limited-use credit is automatically applied first to eligible products and cannot be redirected to ineligible products.

### Applying store credit

Unrestricted store credit is optional at checkout. Turn on **Apply store credit** to use as much as the order allows.

Limited-use credit is restricted to configured products or uses. When an eligible product is present, that credit is applied before unrestricted credit. Credit used by an order reduces the account balance.

### Choosing how to pay

Depending on the cart, choices can include:

- **Pay in Full**; or
- an eligible payment plan.

A payment plan can apply to some items while other items remain payable in full. The cart summary shows:

- the amount due today;
- plan fees;
- future installment dates and amounts; and
- products not covered by the plan.

Review this schedule carefully before proceeding.

### Accepting payment-plan terms

When a payment plan is selected:

1. Open the terms.
2. Scroll through the document.
3. Confirm acceptance.
4. Continue to checkout.

Acceptance is recorded with the order, including the applicable terms version and technical audit information.

### Proceeding to checkout

Choosing **Proceed to Checkout** creates a pending order from the current cart.

If an earlier pending checkout exists for the account, proceeding again cancels that pending order and restores credit or limited-use balances it had reserved before creating the replacement.

If discounts or credit reduce the amount due to zero, the order can complete without opening Stripe payment.

## 10. Checkout and order confirmation

### Checkout

Checkout shows the pending order's products, totals, discounts, credit, and payment schedule. Review the frozen summary before selecting **Pay Now**.

Payment details are collected securely through Stripe. EAC does not receive the full card number.

For a payment-plan purchase, the payment method used for today's charge is saved and attached to that plan for automatic future installments. A pay-in-full checkout may offer saved methods and a choice to save the new method for later purchases.

Do not close the page while payment is actively being submitted. If Stripe declines the payment, the portal returns the order to a state that can be tried again and displays the error.

### Confirmation processing

Payment confirmation can briefly wait for Stripe's secure notification to reach the portal. The page checks the order again automatically. If processing takes longer:

- safely refresh the page;
- check **Billing** for the order status; and
- email [eacdance@outlook.com](mailto:eacdance@outlook.com) if the payment remains unresolved.

Avoid submitting a second payment simply because confirmation is taking a few seconds.

### Order details and receipt

The confirmation page can show:

- order status;
- purchased items;
- purchaser-question answers;
- subtotal, discount, credit, fee, and total;
- paid-today and remaining amounts; and
- future payment-plan installments.

### Assigning newly purchased class seats

After a class order is completed, the confirmation page asks for a student for each purchased seat:

1. Select a student for every seat.
2. Create a student inline if the dancer is not listed.
3. Choose **Save Course Assignments**.
4. Complete any forms created by those assignments.

Seats not assigned here remain available under **My Classes** as open seats. Assignment changes do not change the completed purchase.

## 11. Billing

Billing is divided into five tabs.

### Overview

The overview summarizes:

- available unrestricted store credit;
- the next payment-plan installment;
- open class seats;
- limited-use credit, when available; and
- recent non-canceled orders.

Use the other billing tabs for full detail.

### Orders & Receipts

This tab lists non-canceled orders, including completed orders and orders that may still be pending or failed.

Open a receipt to see:

- items and purchaser answers;
- subtotal and discounts;
- credit used;
- payment-plan fees; and
- totals and payment information.

For a completed order, **Resend Receipt** queues another copy to the account email. Delivery may not be immediate because outgoing mail is processed in the background.

### Payment Plans

Each plan shows its:

- total, paid, and remaining amount;
- status;
- payment method;
- installment schedule; and
- accepted terms and versions.

Choose **View Installments** for individual due dates, amounts, and statuses.

#### Changing a plan's payment method

Choose **Change Payment Method**, then select a saved method or add a new one.

> **Important:** This changes future automatic installments for that payment plan only. Changing the account's default payment method does not automatically change an existing plan.

The action is unavailable once the plan is fully paid.

### Credits & Gift Cards

This tab includes:

- a form to redeem a gift-card code;
- unredeemed gift cards purchased by the account;
- unrestricted store-credit entries; and
- limited-use credit and its restrictions.

An unredeemed gift card displays its code so it can be given to its intended recipient.

> **Important:** Do not choose **Redeem** on a purchased gift card that is intended for someone else. Redemption consumes the card and adds its value to the signed-in account.

Credit entries may have an expiration date. Limited-use credit also identifies what it can be used for.

### Payment Methods

This tab manages saved payment cards.

- **Add Payment Method** securely adds a method through Stripe.
- **Make Default** marks the preferred method for new checkouts.
- **Remove** deletes an eligible saved method after confirmation.

A method cannot be removed while it is assigned to an active payment plan with future automatic charges. Change the method on the affected plan first.

The account default affects new purchases; it does not replace the method already attached to every active payment plan.

## 12. Messages

The page is titled **Email History** and lists sent email addressed to the account's primary email address in the **To** or **CC** fields.

Search by subject, then select a row or choose **View** to open a read-only preview.

This is not a universal inbox:

- it shows sent messages, not messages still queued or messages that failed;
- it does not show active portal announcements from the dashboard;
- it does not include messages where the account was only a blind-copy recipient; and
- it does not show the separate copies delivered to a student's additional email addresses.

An email directed to a student is sent to the account holder as well as every additional email saved for that student. The account holder's copy therefore appears in this history.

## 13. My Profile and security

Open the account menu in the upper-right corner and choose **My Profile**.

### Profile details

The profile can update:

- profile image;
- first and last name; and
- account email.

Save after making changes. The account email is used for signing in and for account-level messages and receipts.

### Changing the password

Enter the current password, then the new password and confirmation. After a successful change, other remembered sign-ins are invalidated while the current session remains active.

Repeated unsuccessful attempts are rate-limited.

### Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication uses an authenticator app:

1. Start setup.
2. Scan the QR code in an authenticator app.
3. Enter the generated code to confirm.
4. Save the recovery codes in a secure place separate from the device.

Recovery codes provide access if the authenticator device is lost. Each code should be treated like a password and used only once.

### Signing out

Choose **Sign out** from the account menu, especially when using a shared computer.

## 14. Important action-effects reference

| Action | Immediate result | Important downstream effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dismiss an EAC notice | Hides the notice for the account | There is no user-facing way to restore it |
| Assign a class seat | Links the purchased seat to a student | Can create required forms for that student |
| Change the assigned student | Moves the seat to another student | Pending form requirements are recalculated; completed forms remain |
| Remove a student from a class | Leaves the purchased seat unassigned | Does not cancel or refund the enrollment |
| Update a medical waiver | Creates and signs a new revision | Preserves the previous completed waiver |
| Update another form | Saves new answers to the existing record | Does not currently preserve a separate signed revision |
| Redeem a gift card | Converts the card to account credit | Consumes the code; the card can no longer be given away |
| Proceed to checkout again | Creates a replacement pending order | Cancels the prior pending order and restores its reserved credit |
| Accept plan terms | Allows plan checkout to continue | Records the terms version and acceptance audit details |
| Make a card the account default | Prefers it for new checkouts | Does not update existing payment plans |
| Change a plan payment method | Updates that plan's future charges | Does not change the account default or other plans |
| Remove a saved card | Detaches an eligible method | Blocked if an active plan still depends on it |
| Delete a student | Permanently removes an eligible student | Available only when the student has no enrollment history |

# Part II: Owner and staff review

## 15. Issues and pain points to address

Priorities below reflect the likely combination of user impact, compliance risk, support burden, and frequency. Items described as “confirm” require an EAC policy decision before implementation.

### High priority

#### 1. Portal-terms acceptance is not recorded

Registration requires the terms checkbox when a portal-terms document exists, but the acceptance is not saved as a legal-document acceptance with the document version, timestamp, IP address, or user agent. A user can therefore be required to agree without EAC retaining evidence of which version was accepted.

There is also a state in which the checkbox is required but no published version is available to link and read.

**Recommended action:** Require a published version before enforcing acceptance and persist an acceptance record for that exact version during registration.

#### 2. Expired, incomplete forms can still be opened and submitted

The **Expired** tab is visually separate, but an incomplete expired form can still route to its edit/sign page. The authorization check verifies ownership but does not reject a form whose valid period has ended.

**Recommended action:** Make expired assignments read-only or inaccessible for completion. Generate a new active assignment when the form is still required.

#### 3. Waiver policy-version evidence is incomplete

Waivers store consent answers and dates, but they do not retain the exact legal-document versions accepted for health and safety or other linked policies. In addition, fields labeled as today's date can be backdated or future-dated.

**Recommended action:** Persist acceptance records or immutable document-version identifiers on the waiver revision, and constrain signature/consent dates according to EAC policy.

#### 4. Account email ownership is not verified

Registration does not require email verification. A signed-in user can also change the account email immediately without verifying the new address or re-entering the current password.

This increases the risk of typos, lost access, or an unauthorized account change and can misdirect receipts or sensitive messages.

**Recommended action:** Enable email verification and require a pending-email confirmation flow for address changes. Consider recent-password confirmation for sensitive security changes.

### Medium priority

#### 5. Essential store actions are easy to miss on mobile

At a 390-pixel viewport, the default list view initially exposes the name and description while price, capacity, and **Add to Cart** are offscreen in the horizontally scrollable table. The card view works better, but new users default to list view.

**Recommended action:** Default to card view on small screens or use a responsive row layout that keeps price and the primary action visible.

#### 6. Global notices overwhelm small screens and duplicate dashboard work

Multiple managed notices and form/enrollment alerts can occupy one or more full mobile screens before the page's actual content. The dashboard then repeats several of the same tasks in **Needs Attention**.

**Recommended action:** Collapse alerts into a compact attention summary, limit simultaneous global banners, and avoid repeating identical actions on the dashboard.

#### 7. Student identity mistakes require staff intervention

Users can change a nickname and additional emails, but cannot correct a student's legal first name, last name, or birth date after creation.

EAC policy is for the family to contact staff rather than edit these identity fields.

**Recommended action:** Add an obvious **Request a correction** action or instruction linking to [eacdance@outlook.com](mailto:eacdance@outlook.com).

#### 8. Enrollment actions disappear without explaining the cutoff

After the assignment-change cutoff, **Change Student** and **Remove Student** simply disappear. A family may not know whether the action is unavailable because of timing, permissions, or a defect.

**Recommended action:** Keep a disabled or informational action visible with the cutoff explanation and a link to [eacdance@outlook.com](mailto:eacdance@outlook.com).

#### 9. Cart decisions are not durable across refreshes

The entered discount, unrestricted-credit choice, and selected payment option are held by the active page state. A refresh or navigation away can make the user repeat those selections.

**Recommended action:** persist applicable cart choices to the cart/session and revalidate them whenever products or quantities change.

#### 10. The additional-email delivery rule is not explained

EAC policy is that an email directed to a student must be delivered to the account holder and every additional email saved for that student. Email History should continue to show only messages actually sent to the account holder. The interface does not explain either behavior.

**Recommended action:** Add helper text beside additional emails. Keep the existing recipient tests and extend them to every future student-directed email path so the account holder and all saved additional addresses remain included.

#### 11. Pending and failed orders have no clear self-service recovery path

Billing can show pending or failed orders, but does not offer an obvious **Resume payment** or **Cancel order** action. Starting checkout again replaces a prior pending order indirectly.

**Recommended action:** Immediately add status guidance linking to [eacdance@outlook.com](mailto:eacdance@outlook.com). Longer term, provide safe resume/cancel actions that explain the effect on reserved credit.

#### 12. Non-waiver signed forms are overwritten when updated

Medical waivers create revisions, but other updateable completed forms modify the existing record and request a new signature in place. The original signed response is not retained as a separate revision.

EAC requires every signed-form update to preserve the prior signed version.

**Recommended action:** Apply the waiver-style immutable revision model to every signed form instead of updating a completed record in place.

### Lower priority and consistency items

#### 13. “Courses / Events” can become an unbounded mixed history

The student detail table is not constrained to upcoming records even though a separate enrollment-history component exists. Over time, the two sections can overlap and the first table can become noisy.

**Recommended action:** Define the first section as current/upcoming activity and leave past records to enrollment history.

#### 14. Redeemed gift-card history is not visible

Billing shows purchased unredeemed gift cards and current credit, but not a clear history of gift cards already redeemed. This can make support questions about a consumed code harder to answer.

**Recommended action:** Add redeemed date, redeemer, original value, and resulting-credit detail where appropriate.

#### 15. The intentional card-only policy is not documented in the interface

EAC intentionally supports only cards for saved payment methods and automatic payment plans. Normal checkout uses Stripe's automatic payment-method capability, while saved-method setup and the billing UI are explicitly card-oriented. Without explanatory copy, users may expect a non-card method seen during one-time checkout to be saveable or eligible for a payment plan.

**Recommended action:** Keep the card restriction, document it beside saved-method and payment-plan controls, and add tests that preserve the intended distinction from one-time checkout.

#### 16. Form completion wording is generic

The primary button on a pending signed form says **Save changes**, which can sound like a draft save even though it completes and signs the form.

**Recommended action:** Use clearer wording such as **Sign and Complete Form**, plus a short statement that submission is final or revision-controlled.

## 16. Features and improvements to consider

These are enhancements rather than confirmed defects.

### Guided onboarding

Add a first-run checklist for creating students, completing waivers, assigning open seats, setting up account security, and reviewing payment information. The dashboard already has the underlying signals needed for much of this.

### Notification center

Combine portal announcements, required actions, payment alerts, and delivery history into a single notification center. Include read/dismissed history and a way to restore dismissed notices.

### Better mobile commerce

Use a mobile-first product card, sticky cart summary, and persistent primary checkout action. The portal is generally responsive, but commerce requires more horizontal exploration than the other areas.

### Store filters and discovery

Add filters for product type, semester, age, instructor, day, and availability. Search alone will become less effective as the catalog grows.

### Calendar export and reminders

Offer **Add to calendar** for individual events or a read-only calendar subscription for a student's assigned classes. Make canceled-event updates flow through the same calendar feed.

### Self-service student corrections

If direct edits are too sensitive, provide a correction request that shows the current value, requested value, reason, and staff approval status.

### Clear status vocabulary

Add tooltips or a short glossary for order, enrollment, form, installment, and credit statuses. Status labels are concise, but their effect on what the family can do is not always obvious.

### Order recovery

Add a guided recovery flow for pending or failed orders, with **Resume**, **Try another payment method**, and **Cancel** actions. Show whether inventory or credit is reserved and until when.

### Downloadable receipts and account statements

In addition to email resend, offer a printable or downloadable receipt and an optional account statement covering orders, charges, refunds, credit, and future installments.

### Payment-method clarity

Document that saved account methods and automatic payment plans intentionally support cards only. Keep one-time checkout's available methods clearly separated so users do not assume every method offered there can be saved or used for future installments.

### Family permissions

Consider whether a family should support more than one adult login with separate credentials and shared student access. This would be safer and clearer than treating additional student emails as informal secondary account contacts.

## 17. Remaining open product question

Should dismissing a managed announcement be permanent for the account, or should users have a dismissed-notice archive?

## 18. Review verification

The review included:

- inspection of the user-panel pages, actions, authorization, and related order/form workflows;
- desktop and 390-pixel mobile walkthroughs using seeded local data;
